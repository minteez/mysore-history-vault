# The Complete History of Mysore

Create a premium educational website called 'The Complete History of Mysore'. It should be a visually stunning, responsive, research-focused website documenting the complete history of Mysore (Mysuru), Karnataka, from ancient times to the present. Organize content into chapters: Geography, Early Settlements, Gangas, Chalukyas, Hoysalas, Vijayanagara Empire, Wadiyar Dynasty, Hyder Ali, Tipu Sultan, British Period, Kingdom of Mysore, Princely State, Unification of Karnataka, Modern Mysuru, Culture, Festivals, Language, Cuisine, Architecture, Palaces, Temples, Economy, Education, Tourism, Famous Personalities, Timeline, Interactive Maps, Historical Gallery, Genealogy, References and Bibliography. Use elegant typography, animations, dark/light mode, accessibility, advanced search, glossary, quizzes, timelines, image galleries, maps and citations. Clearly cite information using Wikipedia as a starting point along with books and reliable historical sources such as B.L. Rice, Srikanta Sastri, Burton Stein, Gazetteers, Archaeological Survey of India and Karnataka government resources. Every article should include references and footnotes. Build a modern homepage with hero section, featured timeline, historical periods, quick navigation, interactive cards, archive section and mobile-first responsive design.
