# The Complete History of Mysore

**The Complete History of Mysore** is an educational and research-oriented digital archive dedicated to documenting the rich and diverse history of **Mysore (Mysuru), Karnataka, India**. The website traces the region's story from ancient settlements and early dynasties through the Vijayanagara period, the Wadiyar dynasty, the reigns of Hyder Ali and Tipu Sultan, British influence, the Princely State of Mysore, Indian independence, the formation of Karnataka, and the development of modern Mysuru.

The project is designed to bring Mysore's history together in one accessible, visually engaging platform. Instead of presenting history as a simple chronological list, the website combines **historical articles, interactive timelines, dynasty information, biographies, maps, architecture, culture, galleries, quizzes, and a research library** to provide a comprehensive learning experience for students, researchers, tourists, and anyone interested in Mysore's heritage.

## What the Website Covers

The website explores a wide range of subjects connected to Mysore and its historical development, including:

* Ancient Mysore and early settlements
* Western Ganga, Chalukya, Rashtrakuta, and Hoysala influence
* The Vijayanagara Empire
* The Wadiyar dynasty and the Kingdom of Mysore
* Hyder Ali and Tipu Sultan
* Anglo-Mysore Wars
* British influence and the colonial period
* Mysore as a princely state
* Industrial, educational, and technological development
* Indian independence and political integration
* The formation of Mysore State and later Karnataka
* The development of modern Mysuru
* Mysore's palaces, temples, forts, monuments, and historic buildings
* Kannada literature and other linguistic traditions
* Music, dance, art, cuisine, and festivals
* Mysuru Dasara
* Important historical personalities
* Tourism and cultural heritage
* Important dates, events, and historical milestones

## Interactive Historical Experience

A central feature of the project is its interactive approach to history. Visitors can explore a chronological timeline covering major events and historical periods, examine different dynasties and rulers, discover important locations through maps, browse historical images, and use quizzes and other interactive elements to reinforce what they have learned.

The website also includes dedicated pages for major historical figures and locations, allowing users to explore subjects individually while still understanding how they connect to the larger history of Mysore.

## Multilingual Support

## Research and Sources

Historical accuracy and responsible sourcing are important principles of this project. Wikipedia may be used as an introductory reference, but it is not intended to be the sole authority for historical claims.

The research material draws from a combination of reputable secondary and institutional sources, including historical books, scholarly publications, government resources, archaeological material, gazetteers, and heritage organizations.

Important reference works and authors include works associated with:

* B. L. Rice
* S. Srikanta Sastri
* C. Hayavadana Rao
* Mark Wilks
* K. A. Nilakanta Sastri
* Burton Stein
* Lewis Rice
* Noboru Karashima

Institutional and reference material may also include resources from organizations such as the **Archaeological Survey of India, Karnataka Government, University of Mysore, Karnataka State Archives, museums, and recognized heritage institutions**.

Where appropriate, individual articles and historical claims include references and citations so that readers can investigate the subject further.

## Main Sections

The website is organized into several major sections:

### History

A chronological exploration of Mysore's historical development from ancient times to the present.

### Dynasties

Information about the major dynasties and political powers that influenced the region.

### Kings & Queens

Biographical profiles, reigns, achievements, reforms, and historical significance of important rulers.

### Architecture & Heritage

Information about Mysore Palace, Jaganmohan Palace, Lalitha Mahal, Chamundi Temple, St. Philomena's Cathedral, Srirangapatna, forts, temples, gardens, and other heritage sites.

### Culture

Exploration of Mysuru's languages, literature, music, dance, festivals, cuisine, traditions, and artistic heritage.

### Timeline

An interactive chronological overview of major events and turning points.

### Maps

Historical and modern geographical information showing important places associated with Mysore's history.

### Gallery

A visual collection of historical photographs, paintings, monuments, architecture, maps, artifacts, and cultural imagery.

### Quiz

Interactive quizzes designed to help visitors test and reinforce their knowledge of Mysore's history.

### Research Library

A collection of references, recommended books, scholarly material, and additional resources for readers who want to explore subjects in greater depth.

### Glossary

Explanations of historical, political, cultural, architectural, and regional terms used throughout the website.

## Project Purpose

The purpose of **The Complete History of Mysore** is to make the history and heritage of Mysore easier to discover, understand, and explore through a modern digital format.

Rather than replacing books, archives, museums, or scholarly research, the website acts as an accessible starting point and educational companion. Visitors are encouraged to consult the cited sources and primary or scholarly materials when conducting serious historical research.
